About the data and its limits

The source

NYC DOT, Bridges & Tunnels Annual Condition Report, 2024 Edition, printed pages 32–64. All 813 unique BINs are loaded. The original PDF was retrieved on October 14, 2026.

Open the original report ↗ · Use Reset, then Download selected records, for the full CSV.

What to keep in mind

This is a 2024 inventory snapshot, with inspection dates from 2017–2024. Eleven structures use a legacy rating; compare them using the rating-basis filter. The unknown borough record stays in the data. PED-coded is a derived group: the source bridge-type text contains PED. It does not establish current permitted use.

Shares use row-level deck-area sums. The report's open-area footer differs from those sums by 46,199.7 sq ft. Conditions describe the reported inventory, not current safety or a causal effect of bridge size.

What the codes mean

PED = Pedestrian · A = Arterial · R = Ramp · O = Off-System · W = Waterway · M = Movable · E = East River · T = Tunnel.

Combined labels retain their components: PED-A = Pedestrian / Arterial; PED-O = Pedestrian / Off-System; AR = Arterial / Ramp. These are report codes, not inferred categories.

NBI-W = National Bridge Inventory Weighted. Legacy exception = one of 11 structures listed in the report as using the earlier rating scale. An older date alone does not imply a legacy rating.

2024 report snapshot; recorded inspection dates range from 2017–2024. The inspection year and full date are shown for each structure.

Definitions: report legend, printed p. 28; rating method and exceptions, printed pp. 20–21.
